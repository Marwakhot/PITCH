Inventra Pitch

1. Unzip this folder (keep index.html and the media folder together).
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome or Edge recommended).
3. Press F for full screen, then any key to start.

Controls: right arrow or click = next, left arrow = back, F = full screen,
M = sound on/off, E = edit text (Esc to finish). Click the demo video to pause it.
Works offline - no internet needed.
